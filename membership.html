How it works

Membership, simply explained.

A monthly membership

Paid each month, or once a year if you prefer. There are no paid tiers: every personal member gets the same care.

A fee agreed with you

Your fee depends on who we look after and what you need. We talk it through on WhatsApp and confirm it in writing before you join.

Purchases approved first

Anything we buy or book for you, such as tickets, gifts, hotels or pieces, is quoted separately. Nothing is paid until you say yes.

Pay from anywhere

When you join, we agree how you pay, whether you live in Bangladesh or abroad.

Anywhere in Bangladesh

Dhaka is our base. We also look after members and their families across the country, through people we trust.

Stop when you like

Membership ends at the close of any paid month or year. Fees already paid are not refunded. Our terms explain the details.

How to join

Four steps, all on WhatsApp.

Anyone can apply, in Bangladesh or abroad. We keep the number of members small so the care stays personal, so now and then there may be a short wait.

  1. Message us

    Tell us who the membership is for and what you would like help with.

  2. Talk it through

    A short conversation, so we understand what you need.

  3. Your proposal

    Your monthly and annual fee, and what it covers, in writing.

  4. Welcome

    Once you agree and the first payment is made, your team is ready.
