About these terms

These terms apply whenever Walvis Concierge (“Walvis”, “we”) arranges something for you, whether you are a member or not. By asking us to go ahead with a request, you agree to them. Walvis is based in Dhaka and serves clients across Bangladesh and abroad.

Membership and fees

Membership is paid monthly or annually, in advance. Your fee is agreed with you on WhatsApp and confirmed in writing before you join.

Membership fees are not refundable once a month or year has started. You can stop at any time, and your membership ends at the close of the period you have paid for.

We may change a fee for future periods. We will tell you at least 30 days before, and you can stop before the new fee applies.

Quotes and your approval

For anything we buy or book on your behalf, we send you a quote first. Nothing is bought, booked or paid for until you have clearly approved it.

Quotes can change with availability, exchange rates, duties and taxes. If a price changes before we commit, we ask you again.

Payment, deposits and third-party costs

The cost of goods and services from hotels, airlines, shops and other providers is separate from your membership fee and is usually paid in advance.

Deposits we pay for you are held by the provider under their own terms. Many are non-refundable. We tell you before you approve if a deposit cannot be returned.

Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel something we have booked, the provider’s own cancellation rules apply. Any refund the provider gives is passed on to you in full.

Work we have already done on a request, and membership fees already paid, are not refunded.

When a provider lets you down

We choose providers carefully and keep a second option ready where we can. If a provider fails, we work to put it right quickly: a replacement, a refund claim on your behalf, or the next best thing.

Providers are responsible for their own goods and services. To the extent the law allows, Walvis is not liable for a provider’s failure, delay or loss, and our total liability for any request is limited to the fees you paid us for it. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

If it’s legal, it’s possible

We only arrange what is legal in Bangladesh and wherever the request is carried out. We may decline any request, and we will tell you why where we can.

Some items, such as antiques, artworks, precious metals, wildlife products, aircraft, boats and vehicles, need import, export or ownership permits. We only proceed where the purchase and delivery are lawful, and duties, taxes and permits are part of the quote.
