Privacy

Your privacy, simply put

Discretion is part of what we do. This page explains what we know about you and what we do with it.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Walvis Concierge is a private lifestyle concierge based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. You can reach us at info@walvisconcierge.com.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, no accounts and no advertising cookies. Its fonts are stored on our own server, so no font service sees your visit.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies or identify you; it tells us, in total, which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from and what devices they use. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy applies to it.

The Walvis Promenade remembers whether you chose its Daylight or Evening look, in your own browser. Nothing about that choice is sent to us.

When you contact us

When you message us on WhatsApp, call or email, we receive what you choose to share: usually your name, number or email address, and the details of your request. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and its terms apply to messages sent through it.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and arrange what you ask for
  • To remember your preferences and important dates, if you are a member and want us to
  • To share the details a hotel, airline, shop or other provider needs to complete your request, and only those details

Health and family details

When we look after a parent or relative, for example with doctor visits or medicine delivery, you may share health details with us, such as conditions, prescriptions or appointments. We treat these as sensitive. We use them only for that request, share them only with the pharmacy, doctor, hospital or caregiver involved, keep them only as long as the care continues, and delete them when you ask. Please only share a relative’s details with their agreement. We coordinate care; we do not give medical advice.

We never sell your information, and we never share it to get something done outside a request you have approved.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are working with you, and for as long afterwards as the law or good record-keeping requires. Ask us and we will delete what we no longer need.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
