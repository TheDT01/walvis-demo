Are you a beach person? · 6 days

The longest sea beach, unhurried.

Sunsets, fresh seafood and a coral island, with a sea-view resort as your base.

  • Day 1. Arrive in Dhaka. Airport welcome, private transfer, hotel check-in and a short briefing.
  • Day 2. Fly to Cox’s Bazar, settle into a sea-view resort, sunset at Laboni Beach.
  • Day 3. Inani Beach and Himchari National Park, then a seafood dinner.
  • Day 4. Saint Martin’s Island, a day trip or overnight stay on the only coral island.
  • Day 5. A slow day. Spa, free time and optional shopping for local crafts.
  • Day 6. Fly back to Dhaka and on to your onward flight.

Saint Martin’s is seasonal, usually November to March. We confirm ferry timings before you travel.

Are you a mountain person? · 6 days

Above the clouds, among the hill people.

Sunrise viewpoints, lakes and village visits in the green hills of the south-east.

  • Day 1. Arrive in Dhaka. Airport welcome, private transfer and a short briefing.
  • Day 2. Scenic journey into the hills of Bandarban and hotel check-in.
  • Day 3. Sunrise above the clouds at Nilgiri, then the Nilachal viewpoint.
  • Day 4. Boga Lake or Sajek Valley, with a visit to an indigenous village.
  • Day 5. A relaxed day with local cuisine and a cultural evening.
  • Day 6. Return to Dhaka and fly onward.

Many hill areas need a permit for foreign visitors. Your concierge arranges this for you.

Are you a mixed person? · 8 days

Heritage, tea, sea and hills.

For the traveller who wants a little of everything, in one well-paced loop.

  • Day 1. Arrive in Dhaka, then a heritage evening through Old Dhaka.
  • Day 2. Sylhet, with rolling tea gardens and the blue water of Lalakhal.
  • Day 3. Ratargul Swamp Forest and Jaflong.
  • Day 4. Fly to Cox’s Bazar, sunset on the beach.
  • Day 5. Inani, Himchari and a seaside dinner.
  • Day 6. Travel into the hills of Bandarban.
  • Day 7. Nilgiri or Boga Lake, then a farewell dinner.
  • Day 8. Return to Dhaka and fly onward.
